# Foodie design brief

Personal culinary journal, one owner, Indonesian public readers. Visit-first narrative and historical accuracy. Public readers browse without accounts; admin records a minimum draft then progressively enriches it.

Approved direction: dark cinematic editorial, charcoal, warm ivory, measured copper; dominant real photography when available. Whitespace/type hierarchy rather than repeated dividers, no filler/fake content. Asymmetric featured visit, quiet task-oriented editor. Variance 5 public / 3 admin; motion 2; craft 7; density 5 admin / 4 public.

Signature: dated editorial title beside the featured photo. No invented reviews or photographs. Empty journal has an honest editorial empty state.

Constraints: mobile 320px+, keyboard controls, 44px internal touch targets, WCAG AA contrast target, native accessible dialogs with focus restoration, no native alert/confirm, shared Blade components, identical sizing within action groups. Ratings nullable; prices explicitly visit total vs item snapshot. Never promise allergen safety.

Learned constraints: approved root specifications are authoritative; no additional style approval needed. The canonical composition and Brand Voice & Microcopy guidance is in `design-system-specification.md` §1. Foodie favors intentional visual richness with simple interaction; whitespace has a purpose, and public/editorial, admin, and utility surfaces have different density. Error pages use the approved Editorial Bold split scene and the supplied kitchen image. Foodie is one writer's culinary journal, not a kitchen, restaurant, delivery service, review community, or objective rating authority; food-service imagery is atmosphere, not a claim about Foodie's operations.
