# Foodie design brief

Personal culinary journal, one owner, Indonesian public readers. Visit-first narrative and historical accuracy. Public readers browse without accounts; admin records a minimum draft then progressively enriches it.

Approved direction: dark cinematic editorial, charcoal, warm ivory, measured copper; dominant real photography when available. Whitespace/type hierarchy rather than repeated dividers, no filler/fake content. Asymmetric featured visit, quiet task-oriented editor. Variance 7 public / 3 admin; motion 2; craft 7; density 5 admin / 4 public.

Signature: a physical memory cabinet opens the journal; editorial photography carries discovery and detail. No invented reviews or photographs. Empty journal has an honest editorial empty state.

Public composition (6 November 2026, playable vending revision): Home is the Memory Archive. Keep the approved 1634×963 cabinet, six windows, screen and recessed tray in one reusable Blade component, never another machine on Explore. LED centers remain (1012,267), (1012,405), (1012,546). The removable glass tube rests in a mechanical cradle at the FRONT LEFT BELOW the cabinet. Power starts OFF; valid RELEASE at the socket snaps, locks and boots it. The reverse valid drop powers down. Independent textured rail, tube, socket, cradle, knurled rotary dial and reset push-button replace the baked-in hardware. The upper dial paginates real published visits in both directions; the lower control depresses for reset. A concrete selected visit prints a labeled photo that opens its actual story. This supersedes the former lamp-pause and surprise-envelope interaction.

Craft read: cinematic culinary journal; variance 7, craft 7, density 4, focal mechanical motion 6 / interface motion 2. No autoplay collections or technical slide counters. Home offers curated selection and a filtered Explore link, never advanced filters. Selection stays local; it does not mutate the homepage URL.

The hero remains mounted while tabs, search, filters, sort, view, pagination and history update via fragments. The two-pane Archive Filter Drawer remains a quiet form surface. Charcoal/ivory/copper and the base atmosphere are shared; crop and light density vary per page. Errors preserve the approved Editorial Bold kitchen scene and config/error-pages.php copy.

Accessible live status is off-screen; contextual first-discovery hint and actionable errors may be visible. The older Explore filter/surprise endpoints remain compatible but do not drive this cabinet. See docs/vending-machine-interactive.md.

Constraints: mobile 320px+, keyboard controls, 44px internal touch targets, WCAG AA contrast target, native accessible dialogs with focus restoration, no native alert/confirm, shared Blade components, identical sizing within action groups. Ratings nullable; prices explicitly visit total vs item snapshot. Never promise allergen safety.

Learned constraints: approved root specifications are authoritative; no additional style approval needed. Foodie is one writer's culinary journal, not a kitchen, restaurant, delivery service, review community, or objective rating authority; food-service imagery is atmosphere, not a claim about Foodie's operations.
